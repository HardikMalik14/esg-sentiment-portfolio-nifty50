01 / WHAT THE RESULTS MEAN

A research snapshot.

The notebook covers 7 May 2025 to 7 May 2026. Its saved portfolio outputs contain five holding months from January to May 2026. The ML models predict next-day returns; none is a reinforcement learning agent.

Return-prediction R² is near zero for every model. Differences between the saved portfolios concern stock rankings, rather than accurate forecasts of daily returns.

02 / THE BIGGEST LIMITATION

Selection looks into the month.

The original notebook chooses each month’s stocks using the average of predictions from within that same month. Those signals were not all available at the start of the holding period.

The dashboard preserves these results and labels them as biased. It does not silently rewrite the original methodology or present these results as a valid live strategy.

03 / HOW THIS WEBSITE WORKS

Recalculated, not retrained.

Capital growth, period returns, volatility, Sharpe and drawdown are calculated from monthly CSV rows. Date range, risk-free assumption, capital and uploaded data affect these calculations.

Headline scoring uses the original VADER and keyword logic. News browsing uses a scored snapshot of the committed articles. The original notebook remains the place to download market data and retrain all four models.

04 / WHAT TO KEEP IN MIND

Five months is a small sample.

    Original prediction errors

    Saved chronological test-set metrics. These belong to the original notebook, independently of any uploaded return CSV.

    Calculation conventions

    Growth: initial capital × product(1 + monthly return). Annualized return: (1 + mean monthly return)¹² − 1. Annualized volatility: sample monthly standard deviation × √12. Sharpe: (annualized return − chosen risk-free rate) / annualized volatility. Drawdown: decline from the running peak, including initial capital. One-month samples have no sample volatility or Sharpe.
